MultiField AI

MultiField AI is a private video-production tool that runs on its owner's own computers. It writes scripts with a local AI model, voices and animates them as 3D short videos, and — after the owner reviews and approves each video — publishes them to the owner's own YouTube channels.

YouTube

MultiField AI uses YouTube API Services to upload the owner's approved videos, to update the owner's channel description and video titles, and to show the owner how the channel's videos perform. Connecting a channel means agreeing to the YouTube Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy.

Who can use it

Only its owner. The tool is not offered to the public, has no sign-up and no other users.
