Terms of Use

Last updated: 4 November 2026

MultiField AI is a private tool used only by its owner to produce videos for, and manage, the owner's own YouTube channels. It is not sold, licensed or offered to anyone else. These terms describe the rules the tool follows.

YouTube

  • MultiField AI uses YouTube API Services. Anyone connecting a YouTube channel to it agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • The tool uses YouTube only through the official YouTube APIs, with the access the channel owner grants on Google's own sign-in page. It does not automate the YouTube website.
  • Only videos the owner has reviewed and approved are uploaded. Nothing is posted to channels the owner does not control.
  • Videos must follow YouTube's Community Guidelines and copyright rules. The tool uses only music, voices and characters the owner made, owns, or may use under their licence.

Data

How the tool handles data is described in the Privacy Policy. In short: data stays on the owner's computers, YouTube data is refreshed or deleted within 30 days, and Disconnect revokes access and deletes the channel's saved tokens and YouTube data.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time with Disconnect in the tool or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

No warranty

The tool is provided as is, for the owner's own use.
