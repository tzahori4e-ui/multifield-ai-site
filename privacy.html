Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

MultiField AI is a private video-production tool. It runs on its owner's own computers and is used only by its owner to make videos for, and manage, the owner's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public and has no other users. This policy explains what the tool does with data, in particular data from YouTube.

YouTube API Services

MultiField AI uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube channel to MultiField AI you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What the tool accesses

When the owner connects a channel by signing in on Google's own page, Google gives MultiField AI permission to:

  • upload videos to that channel (only videos the owner has approved in the tool);
  • read the channel's name, ID and settings, and the channel's public statistics (views, subscribers, likes, comments) and YouTube Analytics reports for the channel;
  • change the channel description and keywords, and the title, description and tags of the channel's own videos.

The tool never sees or stores the Google account password. It does not read e-mail, contacts, other people's channels' private data or anything outside the permissions listed above.

How the data is used

  • To publish the owner's approved videos at the times the owner chose, with the title, description and hashtags the owner approved.
  • To show the owner how the channel's videos perform, so the owner can decide what to make next.
  • To make the channel-page and video-text edits the owner asks for.

Data from YouTube is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone. It is not sent to any other service.

Where the data is stored

Everything is stored on the owner's own computers, in the tool's local database. The sign-in tokens Google issues are stored encrypted. Nothing is stored on a server run by anyone else.

How long the data is kept

  • Data read from YouTube (channel statistics and analytics) is refreshed or deleted within 30 days. The tool deletes any such data that has not been refreshed in 30 days automatically.
  • When a channel is disconnected, the tool cancels its access with Google and deletes the channel's saved sign-in tokens and its YouTube data at once.
  • Videos, scripts and settings the owner created in the tool stay until the owner deletes them.

How to remove access and delete data

  • In MultiField AI: open the channel and press Disconnect. This revokes the tool's access with Google and deletes the saved tokens and YouTube data.
  • In your Google Account: remove MultiField AI at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google's security settings page). The tool can then no longer reach the channel.
  • To ask for any remaining data to be deleted, write to the contact address below.

Cookies and local storage

The tool's window keeps a sign-in session for its owner in local browser storage on the owner's computer. It uses no advertising or tracking cookies and no third-party analytics.
